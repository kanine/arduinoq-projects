Switch Relay

Wiring Requirements

Connect a 1-channel optocoupler relay module to the Arduino UNO Q using 3 wires:

Board PinModule TerminalNotes
D8Trigger (IN)HIGH = relay ON (default jumper)
3.3 VCoil + (VCC)Powers the relay coil (tested at 3.3 V)
GNDCoil − (GND)–

Connect the load to the relay output terminals:

TerminalFunction
NONormally Open — circuit closes when relay is ON
CCommon — one side of the switched load
NCNormally Closed — circuit opens when relay is ON

Optocoupler isolation protects the MCU from switching transients. Coil VCC and IN trigger both run at 3.3 V — tested and confirmed working on this board. Max load: AC 250 V / 10 A  |  DC 30 V / 10 A.
